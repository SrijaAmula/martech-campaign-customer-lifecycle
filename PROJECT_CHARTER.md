# Project Charter

## Project Title
MarTech Campaign Optimization & Customer Lifecycle Transformation

## Business Scenario

A fictional organization runs marketing campaigns across multiple channels
including Email, Social Media, Paid Search and Display.

Campaign performance data is available, but reporting is fragmented across
different platforms. Marketing and Sales teams do not have a single view of
campaign performance or lead conversion across the funnel.

This creates challenges in identifying:

- Which marketing channels generate the best return
- Which campaigns are underperforming
- Where leads are dropping in the conversion funnel
- How marketing spend contributes to revenue
- Which areas require campaign optimization

## Business Problem

Marketing stakeholders currently rely on separate reports and manual analysis
to evaluate campaign performance.

This results in:

- Inconsistent KPI reporting
- Limited visibility into the lead-to-customer funnel
- Delayed identification of low-performing campaigns
- Manual effort in combining campaign and CRM data
- Difficulty making data-driven budget allocation decisions

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to design a Business Analyst-led solution
that provides a governed view of campaign performance and funnel conversion.

The solution will enable stakeholders to:

- Monitor campaign KPIs
- Compare marketing channel performance
- Track Lead → Opportunity → Win conversion
- Identify low-performing campaigns
- Analyze marketing ROI / ROAS
- Support data-driven campaign optimization decisions

## Project Type

Portfolio case study using synthetic data.

No confidential employer, client, or production information is used.
