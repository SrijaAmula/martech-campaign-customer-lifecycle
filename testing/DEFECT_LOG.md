## Defect Classification

### Severity

**Critical**
- Reporting solution is unusable
- Major business process is blocked
- Core KPI calculation is materially incorrect

**High**
- Significant reporting or business-rule failure
- Important KPI or dashboard function produces incorrect results
- Major functionality is unavailable

**Medium**
- Functional issue with a workaround available
- Limited impact on business analysis or reporting

**Low**
- Minor display, formatting, usability, or cosmetic issue
- No material impact on business results

---

## Priority

Priority represents how quickly the defect should be addressed from a business perspective.

- **P1 – Immediate**
- **P2 – High**
- **P3 – Medium**
- **P4 – Low**

Severity and priority should be assessed separately.

For example, a technically minor issue may still have a high business priority depending on stakeholder needs or reporting deadlines.

---

## Defect Lifecycle

The defect lifecycle should follow:

**Open → Assigned → In Progress → Ready for Retest → Retested → Closed**

If the issue is not resolved during retesting:

**Ready for Retest → Retested → Reopened**

---

## Defect Recording Rules

Each defect should include:

- Related UAT Test Case ID
- Related requirement or user story
- Clear defect description
- Expected result
- Actual result
- Severity
- Priority
- Assigned owner
- Current status
- Resolution details
- Retest evidence
- Final closure status

Defects should be based on actual observed behavior.

No defect should be created solely to demonstrate portfolio activity.

---

## BA Role in Defect Management

The Business Analyst supports defect management by:

- Confirming expected business behavior
- Linking defects to requirements and acceptance criteria
- Clarifying business rules
- Supporting severity and priority discussions
- Coordinating with QA, BI, Data, and business stakeholders
- Validating whether the implemented fix meets the original requirement
- Supporting retesting and closure decisions

The BA does not independently own all technical defect resolution.
