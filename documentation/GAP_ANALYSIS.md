# Gap Analysis

## 1. Purpose

This document identifies the gaps between the current campaign
performance reporting process and the capabilities required by
Marketing and CRM stakeholders.

The analysis is based on findings from stakeholder discovery and
the AS-IS process review.

The identified gaps will be used as inputs for business requirements,
future-state process design and solution evaluation.

---

## 2. Gap Analysis

| Gap ID | Current State | Desired Business Capability | Identified Gap | Business Impact | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| G-01 | Campaign data is manually exported and consolidated from multiple marketing platforms | Stakeholders need a consolidated and repeatable view of campaign performance | No standardized process exists for bringing campaign data together | High manual effort and delayed reporting | High |
| G-02 | KPI calculations are maintained manually in spreadsheets | KPIs should use agreed and consistent business definitions | KPI definitions and calculation rules are not centrally governed | Different reports may produce conflicting results | High |
| G-03 | Marketing campaign data and CRM funnel data are maintained separately | Stakeholders need visibility from campaign activity through Lead, Opportunity, Win and Revenue | No consistent end-to-end campaign-to-CRM reporting view exists | Limited understanding of downstream campaign effectiveness | High |
| G-04 | Lead and conversion figures require manual reconciliation between Marketing and CRM | Marketing and CRM should operate using agreed funnel definitions and reconciliation rules | Funnel stage definitions and data ownership are not sufficiently standardized | Stakeholder disagreement and reduced confidence in reporting | High |
| G-05 | Campaign names and source values may require manual standardization | Campaign records should be consistently identifiable across data sources | Common campaign naming and mapping rules are not fully defined | Additional data cleansing and risk of incorrect matching | Medium |
| G-06 | Data discrepancies are investigated manually during reporting preparation | Data-quality issues should be identified and resolved through defined validation rules | Formal data validation and exception-handling rules are limited | Reporting delays and repeated rework | High |
| G-07 | Leadership primarily receives periodic consolidated reports | Stakeholders need timely access to performance trends and underperforming campaigns | Current reporting does not provide sufficiently timely performance visibility | Slower campaign optimization and budget decisions | Medium |
| G-08 | Responsibility for KPI definitions and data issues is distributed across teams | Business definitions, data rules and issue ownership should have clear accountability | Governance and ownership are not consistently documented | Longer decision cycles and recurring reconciliation issues | Medium |

---

## 3. Key Gap Themes

The identified gaps can be grouped into four major themes.

### 3.1 Data Integration

Marketing and CRM information is maintained across separate sources,
requiring manual consolidation and reconciliation.

### 3.2 Data & KPI Governance

Common definitions for KPIs, funnel stages, campaign identifiers
and ownership require stronger governance.

### 3.3 Reporting & Decision Support

Stakeholders lack a consistent end-to-end view of campaign performance
from marketing spend through revenue.

### 3.4 Data Quality & Operational Efficiency

Manual validation, reconciliation and issue resolution increase
reporting effort and delay decision-making.

---

## 4. BA Assessment

The analysis indicates that the business problem cannot be resolved
only by creating a new dashboard.

The underlying gaps include data integration, business-definition
alignment, data-quality controls, ownership and reporting processes.

Therefore, the future-state solution should address both:

1. the information presented to business stakeholders; and
2. the processes and rules used to produce that information.

---

## 5. Next Step

The identified gaps will be translated into prioritized business
requirements and measurable acceptance criteria.

Each major requirement should be traceable back to one or more
business problems or gaps documented during discovery.
