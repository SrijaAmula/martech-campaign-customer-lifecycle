# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## MarTech Campaign Optimization & CRM Funnel Transformation

---

## 1. Document Purpose

This document defines the business requirements for improving campaign
performance reporting and CRM funnel visibility across Marketing and
Sales Operations.

The requirements were derived from stakeholder discovery, current-state
process analysis and gap analysis.

The BRD focuses on the business capabilities required and does not prescribe
the technical implementation of the solution.

---

## 2. Business Background

The organization executes marketing campaigns across multiple digital channels.

Campaign performance data is generated by separate marketing platforms,
while downstream Lead, Opportunity, Win and Revenue information is maintained
within the CRM environment.

The current reporting process requires manual extraction, consolidation,
data cleansing and reconciliation before campaign performance can be reviewed.

This limits the organization's ability to consistently evaluate campaign
effectiveness from marketing spend through revenue.

---

## 3. Business Objective

The initiative aims to establish a trusted and consistent view of
cross-channel campaign and CRM funnel performance so that Marketing and
Sales Operations stakeholders can make better-informed campaign,
funnel and budget optimization decisions.

---

## 4. Business Scope

### In Scope

- Cross-channel campaign performance reporting
- Marketing spend and campaign KPI visibility
- Lead → Opportunity → Win funnel visibility
- Revenue attribution at campaign/channel level
- Standardized KPI definitions
- Campaign and CRM data reconciliation
- Campaign identifier and mapping rules
- Data-quality validation and exception handling
- Reporting ownership and governance

### Out of Scope

- Modification of advertising-platform bidding algorithms
- Creation or execution of marketing campaigns
- CRM sales-process redesign unrelated to campaign reporting
- Predictive lead-scoring models
- Customer churn or lifetime-value modelling
- Replacement of the existing CRM platform

---

## 5. Business Requirements

| BR-01 | The business requires a consolidated view of campaign performance across supported marketing channels. | Reduce dependency on separate platform reports and manual consolidation. | High | G-01 |

---

## 6. Business Success Criteria

The initiative will be considered successful when:

- Marketing stakeholders can review campaign performance across supported
  channels through a consistent reporting process.

- Marketing and CRM stakeholders use agreed definitions for key campaign
  and funnel metrics.

- Campaign performance can be evaluated from marketing spend through
  downstream CRM outcomes.

- Campaign-to-CRM mapping follows documented and repeatable rules.

- Data-quality exceptions can be identified and routed to an appropriate
  owner for resolution.

- Business users can identify underperforming campaigns without repeating
  the existing manual consolidation process.

---

## 7. Assumptions

- Required campaign and CRM data is available from the relevant source systems.
- Marketing and CRM stakeholders will participate in KPI and funnel-definition
  workshops.
- Campaign records can be linked across systems using agreed identifiers
  or mapping rules.
- The portfolio case study uses synthetic data and does not represent
  confidential employer or client information.

---

## 8. Dependencies

Successful delivery depends on:

- Agreement on KPI definitions
- Agreement on CRM funnel-stage definitions
- Availability and quality of source data
- Campaign mapping between Marketing and CRM sources
- Participation of Marketing, CRM, Data/BI and QA stakeholders

---

## 9. Traceability

Each detailed functional requirement, business rule and UAT scenario
created during later phases should trace back to one or more business
requirements in this document.

This enables stakeholders to confirm that the delivered solution addresses
the original business need rather than introducing functionality without
a defined business purpose.
